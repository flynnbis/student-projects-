Unusual Materials

1. Hemp Fabric

Hemp fabric is a type of textile that is made using fibers from the stalks of the Cannabis sativa plant. This plant has been recognized as a source of extraordinarily tensile and durable textile fibers for millennia, but the psychoactive qualities of Cannabis sativa have recently made it harder for farmers to produce this immensely beneficial crop.

2. Wine bottles

Wine bottles can be reused in construction as an eco-friendly building material. This method is often called bottle building or glass bottle construction. Empty wine bottles are placed horizontally or vertically inside walls and held together using materials such as cement, mud, or mortar. The bottles replace some bricks, reducing the amount of new building materials needed.

3. Salt

Salt can be compressed into solid blocks or bricks that are stacked together to form walls and decorative structures. Some buildings use Himalayan salt bricks for interior walls, lighting features, and spa rooms. Salt is also mixed into certain construction materials to improve specific properties such as moisture control.
