Cracked Clay / Aluminum Casting

Cracked clay and aluminum casting can create lightweight but strong structures. Aluminum is durable and recyclable, making it useful for sustainable construction.

Aluminum can be recycled many times without losing quality.

Mycelium Insulation

Mycelium is the root structure of fungi. It can grow into molds and become a biodegradable insulation material. It is lightweight, eco-friendly, and naturally fire resistant.

Mycelium insulation can be grown using agricultural waste.
