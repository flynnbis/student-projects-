Insulated popcorn

Corn kernels are popped and turn into a popcorn which they are pressed into the insulation board then coated with plywood or aluminium. They can then be used like plasterboard or polystyrene. The popcorn panel also are very light and are suitable as an ecological lightweight material. Another advantage is that they can be easily recycled.
