Mycelium

Mycelium is the root-like network of fungi that grows by binding together organic waste materials. It can be grown into molds and then dried to create lightweight, biodegradable building products. As an unusual building material, mycelium is beneficial because it is sustainable, resists fire and mold, and can replace plastics. It also helps reduce waste by turning plant matter into useful building products.
