What is "Translucent Wood"?


Translucent wood is an eco-friendly structural material created by removing the light-absorbing lignin from wood and filling the remaining cellulose scaffold with a transparent polymer.

Transparent wood offers the significant advantage of being five times more efficient than conventional glass. This increase in efficiency is attributed to reduced processing time and decreased consumption of chemicals and energy during the production process. In addition to energy efficiency, transparent wood is environmentally friendly due to its ability to store carbon. It is also utilized for heat storage and is widely used in the construction and aviation industries, particularly in the field of small aircraft.

How is Translucent Wood made?

Delignified and bleached wood materials typically have empty spaces from which components such as lignin and hemicellulose are removed, resulting in a porous structure. To achieve transparency, these pores are filled with a material with a refractive index close to or equal to that of wood.
