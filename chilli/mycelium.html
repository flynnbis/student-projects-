Introduction to mycelium

1. What is mycelium?

Mycelium is the root structure of fungi, used as a building material as it is biodegradable and renewable. At first, it is has a white stringy texture, but is then shaped into bricks

These are some advantages and disadvantages of mycelium:

2. Advantages

Firstly, the material is an excellent insulator, which means it can easily prevent heat loss. Furthermore, mycelium is not just naturally fire resistant, but also very lightweighted. Due to mycelium be purely natural, it is eco-friendly, biodegradable, and carbon-neutral. This not only improves air quality within the building it is used for drastically but also mitigates environmental impacts.

3. Disadvantages

On the other hand, mycelium has its limitations. It will absorb nearly all water in its surroundings, which can provide a humid enviroment. Mycelium is also very weak, so it requires foundations or frames and will not replace concrete. This means that carbon dioxide will still emit due to the need of concrete and the cost will be more since mycelium cannot be on itself
