1. What are Popcorn Panels made from?

They are made from popped corn granules.

2. What type of material is Hempcrete?

It is a lightweight bio-composite building material.

3. What is mycelium?

It is the root-like structure of a fungus.

4. What are Popcorn Panels used for?

They are used as eco-friendly insulation or building panels.

5. What plant is used to make Hempcrete?

The industrial hemp plant is used to make it.

6. What are the thread-like filaments in mycelium called?

They are called hyphae.
