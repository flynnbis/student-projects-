Sea

The sea is a rich and powerful part of our planet. It covers more than 70% of Earth's surface and supports a huge variety of life, from tiny plankton to massive whales.

What is the Sea?

The sea is the large body of salt water that surrounds the continents. It is made up of several oceans, including the Pacific, Atlantic, Indian, Southern, and Arctic Oceans. Seas are important for weather, transportation, food, and the Earth's water cycle.

Marine Life

The sea is home to millions of plants and animals. Coral reefs, fish, jellyfish, sharks, dolphins, sea turtles, and seabirds are just a few of the creatures that live in marine habitats.

Habitats

Ocean habitats vary from shallow coastal areas to deep-sea trenches. Each habitat has its own climate, temperature, and wildlife. Some creatures live in coral reefs, while others survive in cold polar waters.

Human Connection

People use the sea for fishing, travel, recreation, and trade. Coastal towns and cities grow around seas because of their resources and access to global shipping routes.

Why the Sea Matters

70% of Earth's surface is covered by water.
2/3 of all life on Earth lives in the ocean.
30% of the world’s oxygen comes from ocean plants.

Sea Threats

The sea faces many problems today. Pollution, plastic waste, climate change, and overfishing harm ocean life. Warmer waters can damage coral reefs and cause sea animals to move to new areas.

It is important to protect seas by reducing plastic use, recycling, and supporting clean water projects. Healthy oceans help the climate and keep the planet balanced.

Fun Facts
