Astrounauts

Astronauts are people who travel to space. They live and work on space stations and explore other planets.

Habitats

Space habitats are places where astronauts can live and work in space. They provide protection from the harsh conditions of space.

Human Connection

Humans have always been fascinated by space and have dreamed of exploring it. Today, we send astronauts to space and build spacecraft to travel to other planets.

Space threats

Space is not without its dangers. Cosmic radiation, micro-meteorites, and the extreme conditions of space pose significant risks to astronauts and spacecraft.

It is important to develop better protective technologies and safety measures to ensure the success and safety of space missions.
