Hempcrete

This website is about hempcrete


Features


Hempcrete is a biocomposite material made from hemp hurds (which are the woody cores of the hemp plant), water, and a lime-based binder. It is a breathable, lightweight insulating material that is very beneficial towards the environment.



Uses


Hempcrete is used as a sustainable insulating material for walls, floors, and roofs in new, eco-friendly construction. It is also great for retrofitting existing building structures, and restoring historic buildings.



Benefits


•It is carbon negative, which means that it absorbs more carbon dioxide during its lifecycle than it emits.

•It is non-toxic and free from synthetic chemicals, which means that it does not release harmful VOCs.

•It is breathable and mold-resistant, which means that it regulates humidity naturally, preventing mold growth.
