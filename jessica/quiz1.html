What is hempcrete made out of?

Hempcrete is made out of hemp hurds, which are the woody cores of the hemp plant that is mixed with a lime based binder.


What is hempcrete used for?

Hempcrete is mainly used for insulating walls and floors.


What makes hempcrete carbon negative?

It absorbs more CO2 in its life cycle than it emits.
