Mycelium Bricks

Mycelium is the root part of a fungus. It looks like a web of tiny white threads. It is used to grow natural and eco-friendly building materials.

Features

Fire Resistant: Mycelium does not burn easily. This makes it safer than many plastic foams used in buildings today.

Carbon Negative: Most concrete releases CO2. Mycelium takes in carbon as it grows, which helps the planet.

Insulation & Strength: It keeps heat in and blocks noise. It is also strong enough to be made into building blocks.
