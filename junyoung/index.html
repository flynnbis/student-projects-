1. Woodoo: Holz mit magischen Kräften

Woodoo Augmented Wood is a new type of building material created by French architect Timothée Boitouzet.
He improved normal wood by removing lignin and air from the wood and replacing them with a bio-based polymer.
This nanotechnology process created a stronger and more advanced wood material for future buildings.

Properties: As strong as concrete, Fireproof, Translucent, Weatherproof, Bio-based material, Made using nanotechnology
