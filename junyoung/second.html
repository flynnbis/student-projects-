2. Aerographit: Leichtgewicht mit Effekt

Aerographite is an extremely light building material made from a network of tiny porous carbon tubes connected at the nano and micro level.
Its special structure makes it very light, strong, and useful for many purposes.
It is considered the lightest building material in the world.

Properties: 75 times lighter than styrofoam, Can withstand high compressive and tractive loads, Stable and formable
