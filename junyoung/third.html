3. Hanf: Pflanze mit Wirkung

Hanf is currently on everyone's lips. At least literally. The fibres of the plant can also be used
as an excellent insulating material. They are good at absorbing and releasing moisture.
These hemp bricks have a positive effect on the indoor climate, store heat and absorb noise.

Properties: Stainability, Thermal insulation, Sound insulation
