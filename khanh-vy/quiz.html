1. Why is Aerogel often called because of its appearance?

Aerogel is often called frozen smoke or solid cloud because of its ethereal appearance and featherlight feel.

2. Why is Aerogel useful in aerospace engineering?

Aerogel is useful in aerospace engineering because it has exceptional thermal insulation and ultra-low density.

3. What two special properties make Nitinol unique?

Nitinol is known for its shape memory effect and superelasticity.

4. What happens to Nitinol when it is heated after being deformed?

Nitinol returns to its original shape when heated.

5. Name two fields where Nitinol is commonly used.

Nitinol is commonly used in medical devices and aerospace engineering.

6. What happens to Ferrofluid when it is near a magnet?

Ferrofluid forms sharp spikes and unique patterns when exposed to a magnetic field.

7. Name two uses of Ferrofluid.

Ferrofluid is used in speakers, electronic devices, medical technology, and space engineering.
