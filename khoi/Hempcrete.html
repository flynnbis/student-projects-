This page is about Hempcrete

Hempcrete is a light biocomposite made from the core of the hemp plant, water and a lime based binder. Hempcrete is not load bearing and acts as an insulator due to its ability to naturally regulate humidity, improve indoor air quality and provide excellent acoustics. During production, Hempcrete also absorbs massive amounts of Carbon Dioxide, which is more carbon than what is produced during transport. Hempcrete is also 100% biodegradable, requires no pesticides to grow and regenerates the soil it grows on.
