This page is about Popcorn

Popcorn has been repurposed into boards and panels that are light-weight, highly cost-effective, offer natural thermal insulation and provide excellent fire and water protection. This new innovative material was made by granulating and expanding non-edible corn by-products. These popcorn boards and panels are being commercialized by companies as an eco-friendly wall partition and insulation.
