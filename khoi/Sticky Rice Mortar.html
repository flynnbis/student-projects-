This page is about Sticky Rice Mortar

Sticky rice mortar is an ancient Chinese composite building material developed roughly 1,500 years from the Northern Wei Dynasty. It is made by mixing cooked glutinous rice soup with slaked lime. This material significantly increased structural strength, water resistance, and integrity, and is responsible for the long-standing Great Wall of China and many tombs.
