mycelium fungi

Mycelium-based materials, also known as mycelium composites, have evolved from small experimental projects into large-scale industrial applications. Made from the root-like vegetative structure of fungi, they are used as sustainable alternatives to materials in industries such as packaging, construction, fashion, design, and cosmetics. These materials can transform waste into useful feedstock, and depending on whether the mycelium is inactive or still growing, they are classified as either biomaterials or engineered living materials.
