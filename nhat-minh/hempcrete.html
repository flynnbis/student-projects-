Hempcrete

If a haybale got an engineering degree

Hempcrete is a bio-composite made of a mixture of the inner core of the hemp plant and a lime-based binder. The hemp core or “Shiv” has a high amount of silica that helps it bind the lime. Because of this, the outcome is a lightweight material that is able to insulate while weighing roughly seventh or eighth of the weight of normal concrete. Fully cured hempcrete blocks can float in a bucket of water. However, It is not used as a structural material, it only insulates infill between the frame members though it does tend to reduce racking. All loads are carried by internal framing.
