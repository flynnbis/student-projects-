Transparent Wood

Wood, but with pretty clear trust issues.

The main aim of exploring transparent wood was to gain a better understanding of the internal structure of wood. Transparency can be defined as the extent to which an object allows light to pass through it. A non-transparent object such as traditional wood does not let light pass through it so the absorption and scattering of light gives wood its brown color. The way of manufacturing is as follows: wood selection and processing, lignin removal and decolorization, and inserting a transparent polymer into the air spaces.
