Living and Bio-based materials

Bamboo and fiber reinforcement

Recent research suggests that recycled bio-based fibers like bamboo and hemp can replace traditional steel reinforcement in concrete beams, significantly reducing the environmental footprint of structural components.

Bacterial "Healing" concrete

By embedding dormant bacteria (typically Bacillus genus) into concrete, structures can repair their own cracks. When moisture enters a fracture, the bacteria activate and produce calcium carbonate to seal the gap, potentially extending the life of infrastructure by decades (Elgendy et al., 2024; Islam, 2026). Plastic Waste Bricks: Converting plastic waste into construction materials aligns with circular economy goals. While these bricks are generally restricted to non-structural applications due to lower compressive strength, they offer superior water resistance and thermal insulation compared to traditional clay bricks.
