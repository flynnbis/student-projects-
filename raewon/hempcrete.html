Hempcrete

Hempcrete is a sustainable building material made from hemp fibres, lime, and water. It is lightweight, environmentally friendly, and helps reduce carbon emissions.

Advantages

✔ Good insulation

✔ Eco-friendly

✔ Lightweight

✔ Renewable material

Disadvantages

✖ Can be expensive

✖ Not as strong as concrete

✖ Hard to find in some places
