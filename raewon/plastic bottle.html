Plastic Bottle Construction

Plastic bottles can be reused in construction by filling them with sand or other materials. This helps recycle waste and reduce pollution in the environment.

Advantages

✔ Recycles plastic waste

✔ Reduces pollution

✔ Cheap building material

✔ Strong when filled properly

Disadvantages

✖ Takes time to prepare

✖ Not common in modern buildings

✖ Needs careful construction
