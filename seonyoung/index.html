Bio-Inspired Structural Glass

Conventional tempered glass remains highly brittle and vulnerable to dangerous fragmentation, while modern plastics contribute heavily to environmental degradation through microplastic pollution. This project introduces a transparent eco-friendly structural material inspired by biological systems, combining sustainability, flexibility, and advanced impact resistance.
