Plastic:

Today, many industries are introducing new materials by recycling existing ones such as cans, glass, and plastic.Especially, architects and designers are finding creative ways to reuse plastic in order to reduce waste and support sustainability. Recycled plastic can now be incorporated into building facades, where it helps create passive solar shelters and improves both the exterior and interior of buildings. A well-known example is the plastic facade designed by Hyunke Joo, the Korean architect.

❮ ❯

How can plastic be a building material?

Plastic can be used as a building material by reshaping it into useful building products.
Architects and engineers use recycled plastic to create building facades(face of building),
wall panels, roof tiles, and even bricks. Plastic is lightweight, durable and can be waterproofed, which makes it useful in modern architecture.
In some buildings, plastic facades also help control sunlight and temperature so it makes passive solar shelters that improve energy efficiency.
By reusing plastic waste in construction, architects can reduce pollution and support more sustainable building practices.

Advantages:

1) Lightweight and easy to transport while construction
2) Durable & long-lasting
3) Can be recycled & reused
4) Flexible & easy to shape
5)Lower maintenance costs
