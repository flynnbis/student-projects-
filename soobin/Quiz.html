Quiz

1. Why do architects use recycled plastics in buildings?

a) To make buildings heavier
b) To reduce waste and support sustainability
c) To increase pollution
d) To melt buildings easily

b) To reduce waste and support sustainability

2. Why do architects use recycled plastics in buildings?

a) Roof tiles
b) Building facades
c) Bricks
d) All of the above

d) All of the above

3. How can salt be turned into a building material?

a) By freezing it
b) By turning it into solid panels or bricks
c) By mixing it with sugar
d) By burning it

b) By turning it into solid panels or bricks
