Salt:

Salt is an unusual but innovative material that can now be used for printing and construction. Architects and designers are experimenting with salt in large-scale projects because it is both sustainable and environmentally friendly. A huge advantage of salt is that it is a renewable natural resource with a low economic impact during manufacturing. The material is often produced in panels and is known for its porous texture and naturally translucent surface, which can create unique lighting effects in architecture.

❮ ❯

How can salt be a building material?

Salt can be used as a building material by turning it into solid panels or bricks through special manufacturing
and 3D-printing methods. Architects and designers did experiment with salt because it is a renewable natural resource and has a low environmental impact.
Salt panels can be used for walls, decorations, and interior surfaces. One unique feature of salt is its natural translucency, which allows light to pass through and creates
interesting visual effects.

Advantages:

1) Renewable natural resource
2) Low environmental impact
3) Can be recycled & reused
4) Unique translucent appearance
