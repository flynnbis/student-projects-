What is the environment of Antarctica?

Factor Description
Temperature Interior temperatures can fall below −60°C and even below −80°C in some places.
Precipitation Antarctica is technically a polar desert with very little snowfall.
Climate Extremely cold, dry and windy with long periods of darkness.
Wildlife Penguins, seals, whales and seabirds mainly live near the coast.
Ground Conditions About 98% of Antarctica is covered by a thick ice sheet.

What materials are suitable for building homes?

Material Appropriate? Reason
Structural Steel Frames Appropriate Strong enough to withstand high winds and heavy snow loads.
Insulated Steel Panels Appropriate Durable, weather-resistant, and provide excellent insulation.
High-Performance Insulation Appropriate Reduces heat loss and improves energy efficiency.
Triple-Glazed Windows Appropriate Helps retain indoor heat while allowing natural light.
Concrete Inappropriate Can crack in extreme cold and is difficult to construct with in Antarctica.
