What is the environment of desert?

Factor Description
Temperature Often very hot during the day (up to 50°C in some deserts) and much cooler at night.
Precipitation Usually less than 250 mm per year, with infrequent rainfall.
Climate Hot, dry, and arid with low humidity and clear skies.
Wildlife Camels, lizards, snakes, foxes, insects, birds, cacti, and other drought-resistant plants.
Ground Conditions Sandy, rocky, or gravel-covered soil with very little moisture.

What materials are suitable for building homes?

Material Appropriate? Reason
Reflecting Roofing Materiasl Appropriate Reflect sunlight and reduce heat transfer into the building.
Insulated Glass Windows Appropriate Reduce heat transfer while allowing natural light.
Adobe (Mud Bricks) Appropriate High thermal mass absorbs heat slowly and releases it gradually.
Metal Sheets (without insulation) Inappropriate Conduct heat quickly, making the interior very hot.
Dark-Coloured Roofing Inappropriate Absorbs more radiant heat from sunlight.
