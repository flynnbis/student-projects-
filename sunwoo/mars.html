What is the environment of Mars?

Factor Description
Temperature Average temperature is about −63°C. Temperatures can range from 20°C near the equator during the day to below −125°C at the poles.
Precipitation Almost no liquid precipitation. Water and carbon dioxide exist mainly as ice or frost.
Climate Cold, dry, and dusty with a thin atmosphere and frequent dust storms.
Wildlife No confirmed plants, animals, or other living organisms have been found.
Ground Conditions Rocky, dusty soil called regolith, with sand, rocks, craters, and ice deposits beneath the surface in some regions.

What materials are suitable for building homes?

Material Appropriate? Reason
Mars Regolith Bricks Appropriate Can be made from Martian soil, reducing the need to transport materials from Earth.
Steel Frames Appropriate Strong and durable, providing support for habitats.
Radiation-Shielding Materials Appropriate Protect people from harmful solar and cosmic radiation.
Large Glass Structures Inappropriate Offer little protection from radiation and meteorite impacts.
Standard Concrete Inappropriate Requires large amounts of water, which is scarce on Mars.
