What is the environment of ocean?

Factor Description
Temperature Varies from about 30°C in tropical waters to 0–4°C in deep ocean regions.
Precipitation Not applicable underwater, as structures are completely surrounded by water.
Climate Wet, humid, and influenced by ocean currents, tides, and storms.
Wildlife Fish, sharks, whales, dolphins, octopuses, crabs, coral, sea turtles, and many other marine organisms.
Ground Conditions Seafloor may be sandy, muddy, rocky, or covered with coral reefs. Deep ocean floors can be uneven and subject to strong currents.

What materials are suitable for building homes?

Material Appropriate? Reason
Reinforced Concrete Appropriate Strong, durable, and resistant to water pressure when properly designed.
Marine-Grade Steel Appropriate High strength and corrosion resistance in saltwater environments.
Acrylic Viewing Panels Appropriate Stronger than glass and able to withstand high water pressure while providing visibility.
Unprotected Brick Structures Inappropriate Can deteriorate over time due to constant water exposure.
Standard Residential Glass Inappropriate Too fragile to withstand underwater pressure.
