1.What is a salt block used for in buildings?

To absorb moisture and prevent dampness

2.Why do people like salt block rooms?

They provide a unique aesthetic and can help regulate humidity

3.What is one advantage of Hempcrete?

It is a sustainable and eco-friendly building material

4.What is Hempcrete made from?

It is made from the fibers of the hemp plant and a lime-based binder
